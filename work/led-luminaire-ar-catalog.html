An augmented reality catalog where customers browse luminaires, configure them in 3D and see them in AR.

Overview

An augmented reality catalog app for Construlita, made between December 2017 and June 2018.

Customers can browse luminaire families, open each product in a 3D viewer, change its options (finish, mounting type, angle, frame and accessories), toggle the light, read the technical sheet and switch to an AR view that places the luminaire on top of the printed catalog.

I modeled the luminaires in 3ds Max, made the textures in Photoshop and set everything up in Unity, to improve product visualization and customer engagement.

What I did

  • Modeled the luminaires in 3ds Max as product models for the catalog.
  • Textured the models in Photoshop.
  • Integrated the assets in Unity so they work in the 3D viewer and in AR mode.
  • Helped turn a printed catalog into an interactive one, improving product visualization and customer engagement.
