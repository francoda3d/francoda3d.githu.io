About me

Hi, I'm Alan, a Technical Artist and 3D Modeler based in Zapopan, Mexico.

I connect art and technology: modeling, texturing, lighting and integrating full scenes in Unreal Engine, and turning hand-crafted assets and animation into responsive builds in Unity, including UI and gameplay animation.

I started as a 3D modeler making game environments, then led technical art and production teams at Orange Planet Labs and UNIAT, and most recently worked as a Technical Artist at Age of Learning. I like owning the implementation end to end, from asset integration and state machines to UI behavior and performance.

I'm open to new opportunities. Feel free to reach out.
